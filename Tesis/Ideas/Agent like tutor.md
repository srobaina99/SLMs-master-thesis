Focusing on a particular subject or particular concept. Equip the agent with the possibility to evaluate the student with games or autoadaptive questionaries. So that one can have a prompting strategy to show the student harder and harder concepts. 

The role of the teacher might be to gather good material and good evaluation questions of various difficulties so that the student can show the tutor that he is making progress and the tutor can show more and more difficult concepts each time.

**Basic Example**
|sum| - easy sums test -> |multiple digit sums| - hard sums test -> |fractions sum| 

This tool is useful because the teacher can identify the weaknesses of the student and provide bibliografy of questions so the student practice and obtains the level required. It's also useful for those students that are particularly good so if the teacher sees they are doing really good they can send the student to discuss harder concepts with the AI while he focuses on the ones that are at the same pace.


This agent like tutor could be tought as an individual tutor per student or there could be multiple agents in the classroom and the students have to physically move to get from one subject to the next
